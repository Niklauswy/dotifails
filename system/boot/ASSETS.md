# Boot assets · the training tape and the terminal

All boot art is procedural and comes from Wysun's own code and tokens; nothing is downloaded or generated by an
external service.

- `render_assets.py` renders, with Quickshell and the boot shaders (`apps/shell/modules/bootart/BootArt.qml`,
  `shaders/vhs.frag`, `crt.frag`, `filebox.frag`, `mascot.frag`, `phosphor.frag`): `grub/background.png` (the title card on tape),
  `plymouth/background.png` (the office CRT with the header), the project index `card-N.png` and its falling
  cards `flip-N-K.png`, Cold Harbor chosen `click.png` and dissolving `fade-K.png`, the data field
  `field-a/b.png`, the passphrase program `dialog.png` and `pass-K.png`, the cursor, the status lines `status-*.png` and the
  address digits `glyph-*.png`. Everything Plymouth shows goes through `phosphor.frag`.
- The farewell: the mascot's poses `mascot-walk-r/l-N` (12 each), `mascot-turn-r/l-N` (4 each), `mascot-wave-N`
  (16), `mascot-idle-TEMPER-N` (8 per temper: cheer, woe, frolic, dread) and `mascot-hop-N` (10, anticipation to landing), the speech balloons `balloon-*.png`
  and its `shadow.png`. Plymouth moves them around the glass. `--farewell-only` rebuilds these and the two captions.
- It paints with Qt: GRUB's 9-slice frames (`menu_*`, `select_*`, `bar_*`, `bar-fill_*`) and Plymouth's gauge
  `fill.png`; it builds GRUB's sans fonts from DejaVu with `grub-mkfont`.
- It writes `grub/theme.txt` and `plymouth/orbit.script` from their `.in` templates with the Severance token colours
  (plus the card's declared blue), and refuses a GRUB background over 400 KB.

The Wysun mark is original (a globe of ellipses with the name). The style is inspired by *Severance*; no artwork, logo
or font from the series is used. Fonts: DejaVu (license in `grub/FONT-LICENSE`).
